GUIDED swarm consolidation validation — 2026-10-14
=================================================

AI-assisted implementation, migration, analysis and tests. The user performed
physical flights; assistant-run flights used isolated localhost simulators.
No aircraft parameters or firmware changed during this consolidation.

Current interface and scope
---------------------------
guided_swarm.py is self-contained and supports 1–4 vehicle IDs with hover,
forward-return, backward-return, left-return, right-return, three-axis,
circle and diamond. The old guided_flight.py, guided_pair.py and experimental
guided_trajectory.py entry points and their separate documents are removed.

The shared transport, vehicle state/ACK handling and group sequencing now live
in the remaining script. Circle/diamond sampling, telemetry freshness, spacing,
tracking limits, bounded horizontal recovery and landing/takeover handling
are retained. Hover/fixed movements retain the earlier relaxed arrival defaults
(0.30 m, 0.40 m/s, 30 s timeout); paths retain 0.15 m, 0.15 m/s, 8 s. Dwell is
additional to the move timeout; all phases remain subject to the flight limit.
All patterns use the existing group arming policy: one normal arm request per
vehicle, confirmation by ACK or fresh armed state, and no retry after an
explicit rejection. The redundant single-aircraft retry path was removed.
